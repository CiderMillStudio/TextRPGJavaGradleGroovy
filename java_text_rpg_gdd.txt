Goals of RPG Game:

1. Toggle between WORLD_EXPLORATION, LOCAL_EXPLORATION, COMBAT, TRADE, and CAMP
    WORLD_EXPLORATION:
        you are presented with a map overview with ASCII art showing the different world regions, and you see your
        player character's location as well. You can move your player character using the arrow keys.
    LOCAL_EXPLORATION:
        Local_Exploration is a text-based adventure using a mix of ASCII art (which portrays each PLACE_OF_INTEREST)
        and text narration and choice selection to
        progress through a given area. Examples of LOCAL_EXPLORATION areas include VILLAGES (shop, get quests, mingle),
        DUNGEONS (Combat, traps, loot), RUINS (lore, charms, respite), and ENCOUNTERS (bandits, wandering merchants,
        strange omens, foraging/hunting opportunities)
    COMBAT:
        Combat is a turn-based system relying on rolling a d12 to attack and take actions. Will be able to take actions
        such as FIGHT, USE_ITEM (or change weapon),
    TRADE:
        Can occur within a SHOP (for example in a VILLAGE), or via a WANDERING_MERCHANT. Player can exchange COINS for
        ITEMS (buy) or ITEMS for COINS (sell).
    CAMP:
        When player is feeling weary (low hp), can MAKE_CAMP and engage in CAMP_EVENTS, including HUNT, FISH (if near
        body of water), FORAGE, COOK, CRAFT, REST.

2. Player may accept QUESTS (through the TALK mode) that have one or several objectives
    (some quests must have objectives completed in a certain order, others may be completed at will)
3. This RPG System has 4 stats: Strength, Dexterity, Charisma, and Intelligence
4. There are 6 classes that come with various tools and Stat Bonuses
    Warrior: +2 to initial strength, +1 to strength per level up by default
        Wields Sword, draws from Strength to attack with sword
    Archer:  +2 to initial dexterity, +1 to dexterity per level up by default
        Wields Bow, draws from dex to attack with bow
    Rogue:   +2 to initial dexterity, +2 to initial charisma, +1 to either dexterity or charisma per level up (alternates)
        Wields dagger, draws from dex to attack with dagger
    Wizard:  +2 to initial intellect, + 1 to intellect per level up by default
        Casts with Staff, draws from intellect to cast
    Bard:    +2 to initial charisma, +1 to charisma per level up by default
        Casts with Bard_Focus (item of bardic inspiration, could be an instrument, a book, a puppet), draws from charisma
    Monk:    +2 to initial strength, +2 to initial intelligence, +1 to either strength or intelligence per level up (alternates)
        Wields MonkStaff, draws from strength and intelligence to attack with MonkStaff
    Artificer: +2 to initial intelligence, +1 to intelligence per level up by default
        Casts wtih Artificer_Focus (item of Artificer_Focus, could be a contraption, a hacked-magical item, an
        altered-tinkered-bow), draws from intelligence and a stat determined by the Artificier's item of inspiration

GAME ENGINE LOGIC

Game initialization:
- Run through game initialization scripts
- Load Data if applicable
- Enter Start Menu GUI
- Initialize Chosen Game



Game Loop (12 FPS to keep things running fast):
- For any GAMEOBJECTS who are brand new to the Game, run their OnAwake methods
- For any GAMEOBJECTS who are brand new to the Game, run their Start methods
- Run through the Update method for each GAMEOBJECT 
- Solve Collisions, place GAMEOBJECTS
- Solve which GUI menus are open, and their states
- Render Visusals (print GameObjects and Menus on new screen)
- Render Audio (if needed)
- Listen for input from player



Visuals: 

- Game resolution 90 x 30 ASCII characters (row 1 is border, 2-23 is screen, row 24 is the border, and rows 25-29 is GUI, and row 30 is border
- Each character represents a PIXEL. 
- PIXELS have three variables: CHARACTER, COLOR, LAYER (int) (thus, one sprite may contain pixels of different layers.
- SPRITES consist of HEIGHT, WIDTH, X_location (of top-left most pixel), Y_location (of top-left most pixel) (0,0 is located at the top-left of the screen),
	 and a PIXEL ARRAY defining what the Sprite looks like.
- SPRITES can be assigned to GAMEOBJECTS

Collisions:
-  COLLISION_MAPS can be assigned to GAMEOBJECTS which dictate which pixels within a GAMEOBJECT are collidable.
-  COLLISION_MAPS consist of an int array indicating LEVEL of collidability for each pixel:
	0=non-collidable, 1=collidable_on_ground_level, 2=collidable_while_jumping, 3=collidable_impassable
	Where 0 can be walked through, where 1 can be jumped or flown over, where 2 can only be flown over, and 3 cannot be passed (without teleportation, that is?). 
- Thus, if a GAMEOBJECT has both a SPRITE map and a COLLISION_MAP, and you have two GAMEOBJECTS approaching each other, whether or not the GAMEOBJECT can proceed forward depends on if their collision maps meet. Ghosts might have a Collision map of 0 entirely, whereas Skeletons might have a collision map of 1. A little home might have it's perimeter as collision map 2 but the interior might be collision map 0.
- COLLISION_EVENT is when a GAMEOBJECT moves such that it becomes directly adjacent with another collidable GAMEOBJECT. At this point, movement is restricted and movement is denied in that direction.


PlayerController:
- Will listen for input regarding whether the player wishes to move or take an action.
- WASD or Arrow keys to move player and to move through UI menus
- ENTER to make a choice in UI menus and navigate to submenus
- E to INTERACT with GAMEOBJECTS (Interactables)
- M to toggle the GAME_MENU (shows inventory, quests, fast travel map)
- ESC to toggle the PAUSE_MENU (shows "Save and Exit", "Settings", etc...)
- T to toggle betweeen GAMEOBJECTS on screen, which you can then press E and interact with (either from a distance or up close, and this will cause variation in how you may interact with them).
- Fastkeys: 
	- F -> Fire/attack with primary weapon
	- T -> Talk (E but specific for TALKING) 
 

GameManager:
- GameManager is a Singleton Class that contains instances of important manager classes like:
	- PlayerManager (contains InventoryManager, PlayerStatsManager, etc...)
	- SceneManager (contains the current scene, provides scene information to the visualization renderer. Loads new scenes in, is sure to save each scene's state to ensure persistence).
	- QuestManager (Contains that state of each quest)
	- NPC_Manager (contains the states of each NPC)
	- CalendarManager (keeps track of the in-game date and time, as certain events only happen at certain times of the day/year) 
	- etc...

CLASSES


ENTITY: An Entity is anything with stats (HP, Strength, Dex, Charisma, Int) that the player may engage with. An Entity also has a ANSII Symbol, a ANSII Symbol Color, a descriptor, and other attributes that other living things would have (An attack sound effect, a damage sound effect, a die sound effect). Subclasses of Entity may have additional functionality (for example, Merchant can set up TRADE mode with the player, and Animals can be PET via pettable interface, etc...) 

GameObject (something that can appear on the screen as something within the game, has coordinates, ...)

GameObject 
--> Entity -->
	Player (additionally has controls, inventory, level tracking, and other things that only the player has)
	NPC --> (additionally has loot, exp on slay, interactability)
		EntityHostile --> 
			Skeleton
			Bandit
			Wolf
			GiantRat
			...
		EntityFriendly --> (additionally has ways for player to command the ally by setting ALLY_MODE via the Commandable Interface)
			Pet (Pettable, Commandable, Combattable)
			PartyMember (Commandable, Combattable)
		EntityNeutral (neutral entity) -->
			Villager
			Merchant
			AnimalNeutral
			DungeonDweller



(Non-GameObjects)

Scene -->
	
	WorldScene (for WORLD_EXPLORATION GameMode, allows player to simulate fast-travel between different Place(s)OfInterest) 
		Note that the WorldScene is foggy and obscured, but reveals more and more as you explore, like Elden Ring Map Fog) 
	
	PlaceOfInterest --> (for LOCAL_EXPLORATION GameMode, can walk around these places, find interesting things, etc...)
		Village (Shops, a Bank, maybe an Inn, maybe a farm) 
		Dungeon (explore, hostile, multiple levels deep)  
		Ruins (explore, non-hostile, solve riddles and such, acquire charms, players are encouraged to come back to these over time and apply what they've learned)
		NaturalWonder (makes for an ideal place to camp...)
		FertilePlains (can farm here, might require you to liberate these plains from crooked bandits, and return to these locations periodically to harvest your crops)

INTERFACES (contracts)
Interactable (for objects or things that don't speak, but provide some sort of interactability/info)
Talkable (for Entity who can speak)
Combattable (for Entity who can engage in combat, and hence can be slayed)
Lootable (for Entity or Thing which can be looted for items and coins)
Shoppable (for Entity who player can Shop with)
Tamable (for AnimalNeutrals who can be Tamed into Pets)
Commandable (for Entity that the player can command, usually allies in battle)
